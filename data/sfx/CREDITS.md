# Sound credits

Generated by `tools/gen_sfx.py`. Weapons, explosions, loops (rotor, engine, wind, fire) and stingers are synthesized (own work).
Processed from Kenney sound packs (CC0, https://kenney.nl): Interface Sounds (clicks, select, confirm, error, open/close, bong, maximize, glitch),
Impact Sounds (metal/plate/mining/plank impacts), Sci-fi Sounds (laserLarge, forceField, slime, explosionCrunch).
