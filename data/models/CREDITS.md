Most models are generated by `tools/gen_models.py`. The files below are converted from public-domain (CC0)
models by `tools/import_models.py` (normalised, decimated, split into hull/turret/barrel, weathered textures,
team-colour masks or remapped onto our palette). Thanks to the authors.
